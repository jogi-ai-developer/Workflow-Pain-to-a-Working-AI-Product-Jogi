---

# PRODUCT DIRECTION

Do NOT build a generic:

"AI Funnel Analyzer"

Instead, explore this product concept:

> A diagnostic decision-support tool that uses deterministic funnel analysis to identify meaningful drop-offs, then uses AI to generate evidence-based hypotheses and recommend what the user should investigate or test next.

Core principle:

DATA tells us WHERE the problem is.

LOGIC determines WHETHER the drop is meaningful.

AI helps reason about WHY it might be happening.

HUMAN decides WHAT to do.

Do not claim that the system can know the true causal reason for a funnel drop.
